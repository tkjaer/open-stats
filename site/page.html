open-stats

Approximate page-load counts for a few small sites, published openly. Each page load sends one request with only the page language, and no cookie or ID. The server necessarily sees the visitor's IP address; it is used only in memory, for the country and the rate limit. What is collected, how and why: github.com/tkjaer/open-stats. The numbers are approximate: reloads count, and visitors with Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track or an ad blocker aren't counted. Days are UTC.

Countries per day

A country with too few page loads on a day is counted under “other” for that day, so a dash means “fewer than that, included in other”. A day with too few page loads in total isn't broken down at all. The thresholds are given for each week. There are deliberately no weekly totals per country: with them, the hidden days could be worked out.
